## Use fasttrop

Read the results of a fit and know the limits of the method.

- [How to read your
  results](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/read-your-results.md):

  What each number in the output of fasttrop means, and what to do when
  fasttrop gives a warning message.

- [Use
  covariates](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/covariates.md):

  How fasttrop adds covariates to TROP, the two procedures for the
  coefficients, and how we checked them.

- [Effects by group and many
  tests](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/event-studies.md):

  How to get the effect for each event time, how to keep some treated

  study](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/large-studies.md):

  How to make the treatment, the target, and the event times from start

  bootstrap with a checkpoint, and read the diagnostics of the draws.

- [Choose a tuning
  grid](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/tuning-grid.md):

  Set the grid for the units of the outcome. Read a grid warning and

- [What TROP can and cannot
  do](https://farach.github.io/fasttrop/articles/limits.md):

  The limits of the method and of this package in plain words, and good
